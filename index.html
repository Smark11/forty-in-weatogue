Four things that change the plan

HBH's bouncers are kids only

HBH's rental contract says adults are "strictly prohibited" from its inflatables. HBH is still a good choice for the tent, chairs and cotton candy. For a bouncer adults can use, go to Bounce About, CT Inflatables or Party Patrol.

The tent probably needs a permit

In Connecticut, tents over 400 sq ft need a building permit and Fire Marshal approval. A 20×30 is 600 sq ft. With heaters and 50 guests, no exemption applies. Ask the tent company to pull the permit.

Deposits lock in almost right away

HBH and BreMar refund deposits only up to 14 days before the party, which is Sun Sep 27. Bounce About lets you cancel free up to 7 days ahead. Book the pieces you're sure about first.

Bounce before the drinks

Inflatable contracts bar anyone who has been drinking. Schedule bouncer time early in the party, and give the kids the last hour.

Tent & driveway

Get the 20×40 if everyone sits down

A 20×30 fits 50 people standing, but only about 35 seated at round tables. If the party includes a sit-down meal, cake table and a bar, a 20×40 gives you the room.

Putting it on the driveway

  • Use a frame tent. Frame tents stand on their own and can be held down with water barrels or concrete weights. Pole tents need stakes in grass.
  • Measure first. You need a flat 20×40 (or 20×30) area plus a couple of feet on each side for the weights. Driveway slope matters, so mention it on the call.
  • Ask about the weight fee. Tucker's charges for 8 water barrels and you supply the hose. Party Patrol charges a pavement fee. Meriden includes weights. BreMar and Expert say frame tents work on pavement but don't list a fee.
  • Staking in the yard? Whoever drives the stakes has to call 811 (Call Before You Dig) two business days ahead. For Friday setup, that's Wednesday Oct 7. The tent company usually does this.
  • Heat it safely. Rent a forced-air heater ducted into the tent (BreMar $180). Propane patio heaters are for open air, so put them outside the tent. Keep fire pits at least 20 ft from the tent.
  • Permit contacts. Simsbury Building Dept 860-658-3234 and Fire Marshal 860-658-1971.
  • Plan for power. A cotton candy machine, a blower and lights each need their own circuit. Taylor says no extension cords for its cotton candy machine. Bounce About rents a generator for $100.

Polish food

Who can cater pierogi for Sunday

Researched Oct 1, 2026. Katie asked for Polish comfort food, and Little Poland on Broad St in New Britain is about 30 minutes away. The catch: most of these kitchens close on Sundays, so the plan is either Belvedere Cafe on party day or a Saturday pickup you reheat in chafing dishes.

Sunday is the problem

Taste of Poland, Euro Plate and the Pierogi Queen store are all closed Sunday. Only Belvedere Cafe (11am to 7pm) is confirmed open on Oct 11. Everyone else means a Saturday pickup and a reheat.

Call this week

Ten days out, order whichever kitchen says yes first. Ask for a tray quote for the list below, and taste a sampler plate when you pick up the order confirmation if you can.

Reheating works

Pierogi, kielbasa, gołąbki and bigos all reheat well. Rent two or three chafing dishes with the tent order (BreMar and Tucker's carry them) and pan-fry the pierogi in butter before guests arrive.

What to order for 25 adults and 10 kids

  • Pierogi, 10 to 12 dozen mixed, mostly potato & cheese
  • Kielbasa with sauerkraut, one large tray
  • Gołąbki, 30 to 36 pieces
  • Bigos, one large tray
  • Potato pancakes with sour cream and applesauce
  • Cucumber or beet salad for something fresh
  • Kids: plain pierogi plus mac and cheese or pizza
  • Dessert: pączki, szarlotka and a sheet cake from Roly Poly

Budget roughly $600 to $900 for trays. Pierogi Queen's staffed service adds about $6 per person plus food.

Questions for the food calls

  • Can you do Sunday Oct 11 pickup or delivery to Weatogue? If not, what time Saturday?
  • How many people does each tray feed, and what does the list above cost?
  • Do trays come in foil pans that go straight into a chafing dish?
  • What is the deposit, and when do you need the final count? RSVPs close Oct 4.
  • Can you add a vegetarian option, like sauerkraut & mushroom pierogi and placki?
